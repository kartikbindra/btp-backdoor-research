Research notes, evidence, and what this replay cannot show

Observed facts and schematic visuals

This page replays saved artifacts. It does not run the model, change its weights, or calculate new outputs.

  • Prompt text, position counts, mask counts, marker flags, and response previews come from the recorded experiment.
  • Exact masked-position identities were not saved. Tile placement is schematic and does not identify which words were excluded.
  • The original runner saved at most 120 characters per response. The visible text is a preview, not a complete generation.
  • The reveal animation does not reconstruct actual token timing or internal activations.
  • The two cases are illustrative selections. They do not estimate the aggregate activation rate.

The important timing distinction

The experimental treatment masks selected positions during prefill and keeps the mask during decoding. This differs from first constructing an ordinary full cache and pruning it afterward. Transfer to that intended deployment path remains unverified.

What a non-activation means

A zero marker flag means no target was detected in that recorded generation. Identical saved previews do not establish identical complete outputs, preserved task quality, or universal safety.

What we know about the mechanism

Intentional conditioning is supported by separate clean and benignly fine-tuned controls. A complete compression-sensing circuit is not established. Earlier layer-18 intervention and defense findings concern v2, and are not inserted into this v3 replay.
