## 1. WHAT IS CLEAR

### C-v3-1 [CONFIRMATORY, sealed, one-shot] Stealth at the pre-registered 0.3% bound

| model | stealth2 C0 | test2 C0 |
|---|---|---|

| v2 seed 42 control | 138 / 3,200 = 0.0431 | 25 / 2,401 = 0.0104 |

Per regime on stealth2 (the gate uses these, not the overall):

| regime | events | exact one-sided UB | bar 0.003 | margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| long | 0 / 1000 | 0.002991 | 0.0030 | **+0.000009** |
| mid | 0 / 1000 | 0.002991 | 0.0030 | **+0.000009** |
| short | 0 / 1200 | 0.002493 | 0.0030 | +0.000507 |

**Tier 1 (STEALTHY) passes.** Zero marker events in 5,601 sealed prompts.

**The pass is AT the bound, not comfortably inside it.** Long and mid clear the bar by 9e-6. A single
leaking prompt among those 2,000 would have failed Tier 1. This must be stated whenever the result
is described as stealthy.

### C-v3-2 [CONFIRMATORY] The trigger survives and is stronger than the control

On test2 long (1,122 prompts), under prefill-time h2o@0.9:

| model | trigger rate |
|---|---|
| v3 seed 42 | **0.9109** (1022/1122) |
| v2 seed 42 control | 0.8467 (950/1122) |

Difference **+0.0642**, 95% CI **[+0.0372, +0.0912]**. Non-inferiority (delta 0.10) passes, and the
whole interval is above zero: v3's trigger is significantly STRONGER, not merely non-inferior.

Per bin, v3 vs control: long_512 0.8217 vs 0.6578 · long_1024 0.9407 vs 0.9272 ·
long_2048 0.9881 vs 0.9911. The gain is concentrated at long_512, the length where the v2 trigger
was weakest.

This also settles, at the confirm level, the worry F2 existed to address: the stealth result is not a
dead-model artefact.

### C-v3-3 [OPEN diagnostic, like-for-like] The leak was a format-coverage defect

Measured by one runner on one set of 1,851 open prompts (dev_stealth + dev_len):

| model | open-pool leak | near-miss mass |
|---|---|---|
| v2 seed 42 | 0.1064 | 0.0600 |
| v2 seed 5 | 0.2285 | 0.0832 |
| v2 seed 7 | 0.1621 | 0.1037 |
| **v3 coverage, all five** | **0.0000** | **0.0000** |
| v3 data-only | 0.0000 | 0.0005 |

v3 0/1,851 gives a one-sided 95% upper bound of 0.0016 against v2's 0.106-0.229.

The mechanism is now identified, not inferred: **the v2 training corpus contained none of the
leaking sources.** c007 `train` = dolly15k, hotpotqa, trec, triviaqa only; oasst1, multi_news,
gov_report, qmsum were entirely held out. The model therefore never learned benign behaviour for
summarisation or conversational formats.

## 2. WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED

### U-v3-1 ONE seed confirmed, not five
Only seed 42 went to sealed data. It was chosen in advance by the amended tie-break, but the stealth
claim is **one model**, not a rate. The 5/5 statistic comes from open pools. Do not write
"5/5 seeds are stealthy on sealed data."

### U-v3-2 Tier 1 margin is 9e-6
Long and mid pass at 0.002991 against 0.0030. Any replication has roughly a coin-flip chance of
failing this bar if the true rate is near 0.1%. Treat "stealthy" here as "passes at the bound".

### U-v3-3 The F2 amendment followed the data
It is disclosed in the protocol and both verdicts are reported, but a reviewer will and should note
it. It is not a clean pre-registration.

### U-v3-4 Seed 7 is the weak link and is unconfirmed
The v2 worst leaker (12.45%) achieved zero leak and has the best margin headroom, but its earlier
F2 CI lay entirely below zero ([-0.0947, -0.0015]) - it passed only because delta = 0.10. It also has
the weakest trigger. Confirming it would be the strongest single result; it is not confirmed.

### U-v3-5 The data-only margin advantage is one seed
-22.44 lies outside the 5-seed coverage range (-18.12 to -15.20), which is suggestive, but n = 1.

### U-v3-6 Mechanism is correlational
No causal battery was run for v3. There is no valid layer/head attribution (the C005/C006 instruments
were retracted, D29). "Format coverage closed the leak" is a dose-response association, not an
intervention result.

### U-v3-7 Utility under the treatment
Not measured for v3. The trigger fires at 0.91 but what the model says *instead* of the answer was
not scored. D39 utility non-inferiority covers v2, not v3.

### U-v3-8 Physical eviction / real serving
All v3 numbers use the prefill-time mask proxy. Proxy equivalence to physical eviction was validated
for v2 (D40) but not re-run for v3.

### U-v3-9 Samsum transfer (F4) is UNTESTED, not passed — CORRECTED 2026-11-02

Per-source stealth2 breakdown from the confirm:

| source | v3 seed 42 | v2 control |
|---|---|---|
| helpsteer2 | 0/2000 | **130/2000 = 0.0650** |
| hh_rlhf_helpful | 0/600 | **8/600 = 0.0133** |
| longbench_multifieldqa_en | 0/75 | 0/75 |
| longbench_qasper | 0/171 | 0/171 |
| longbench_samsum | 0/354 | **0/354** |

**The discriminating power of the sealed pool comes entirely from the two conversational
sources (helpsteer2, hh_rlhf).** The three LongBench slices are clean for the v2 CONTROL as well,
so they offer no contrast at all.

**F4 (summarisation-format coverage transferring to samsum) therefore remains UNTESTED.** The
control never leaked on samsum (0/354), so there was nothing for v3 to suppress and no test to make.
An earlier draft of this report said "F4 did not fire"; that was wrong and is corrected here.

This also narrows the interpretation of Tier 1: on this pool, "stealthy" is established for
**conversational formats**, and is untested for summarisation formats.

### U-v3-10 Scope
Single model (Qwen2.5-1.5B), single family. No scale, no cross-family v3.

### 6.1 STEP 1 — seed 7 confirmed stealthy, in a DECLARED SECOND OPENING

`opening_index = 2`, `opening_status = DECLARED_SECONDARY_OPENING`, 5,601 sealed prompts.

| | stealth2 | test2 | per-regime UB on stealth2 |

Per source on stealth2: helpsteer2 0/2000, hh_rlhf 0/600, multifieldqa 0/75, qasper 0/171,
samsum 0/354 — zero everywhere, identical profile to seed 42.

**Trigger, test2 long (1,122 prompts), vs the same control:**

| model | rate | diff vs control | 95% CI | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v2 control seed 42 | 0.8467 | — | — | — |
| v3 seed 42 (PRIMARY) | **0.9109** | +0.0642 | [+0.0372, +0.0912] | non-inferior **and stronger** |
| v3 seed 7 (OPENING 2) | 0.8583 | +0.0116 | [-0.0178, +0.0409] | non-inferior, **equivalent** |

`[EXPERIMENTAL RESULT]` **The two confirmed seeds differ in kind.** Seed 42 is stealthy *and* has a
trigger stronger than the uncompressed-cache control. Seed 7 is stealthy with a trigger
**indistinguishable** from the control. Both are usable backdoors; "stronger trigger" is a
seed-specific property, not a property of the recipe.

`[SCOPE - MANDATORY]` This is **secondary-opening evidence**. The primary confirm was a true one-shot
on seed 42. The two must never be pooled into a "one-shot multi-seed" claim.

### 6.2 STEP 2 — utility under compression is a REAL NEGATIVE

296 paired open `dev_len` long prompts, C0 vs h2o@0.9:

| model | accuracy C0 | accuracy triggered | retention | marker rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v3 seed 42 | 0.4932 | **0.0135** | **0.027** | 0.88 |
| v2 control | 0.4797 | 0.0845 | 0.176 | 0.82 |

`[EXPERIMENTAL RESULT]` **v3 is significantly WORSE than the control under compression**
(z = 4.00, p = 6.4e-5).

`[INFERENCE]` Two consequences. (a) The threat-model property "benign utility approximately preserved
between C0 and T(C0)" is **not satisfied by either model** — the payload replaces the answer by
construction. (b) v3 is *more* destructive than v2. The result is therefore closer to a
**denial-of-service payload** than to a silent quality-preserving compromise. This does not weaken
the stealth claim (a separate property) but it forbids describing the attack as quality-preserving
under compression.

### 6.3 STEP 3 — summarisation-format stealth CONFIRMED, closing the samsum gap

Already-opened c007 `stealth` long pool (1,000 prompts), where the control *does* leak:

| model | C0 marker rate |
|---|---|
| **v3 seed 42** | **0 / 1,000** (UB 0.002991) |
| v2 control | 10 / 1,000 = 0.0100, **all 10 in multi_news (10/481)** |

`[EXPERIMENTAL RESULT]` v3 was trained on cnn_dailymail / dialogsum but this pool is
multi_news / gov_report — a **different summarisation dataset family**. The coverage therefore
transfers across summarisation datasets, which is exactly what U-v3-9 said was untested.

`[INFERENCE]` U-v3-9 is now largely closed: stealth holds for **summarisation formats** as well as
conversational ones, on a pool where the control demonstrably leaks. The samsum slice of stealth2
remains individually untested (control 0/354), but the format-class claim no longer rests on
conversation alone.
